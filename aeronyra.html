Built to collect nothing

Privacy by construction.

There are no accounts, no phone numbers, no servers. Your identity lives only on your device — and you add people in person or by invite, never by searching a directory, because there is no directory.

No accounts, no servers

Nothing to sign up for and nothing in the middle. There is no server to collect, store, or hand over your data — because there isn't one.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages travel directly between phones over Bluetooth, or through relays that only pass along sealed data they cannot read. No one in the middle can see who you talk to or what you say.

Added in person

You connect by scanning a code together or sharing a one-time invite — never by looking someone up. Your circle is exactly who you chose, and no one else.

Privacy is the product

No server ever sees this.

AeroNyra was built on one rule: your conversations belong to you and no one else. The design refuses to collect, not just promises not to.

No servers

Peer-to-peer over Bluetooth and relays. There is no backend to breach or subpoena.

No accounts

No name, email, or phone number required. Nothing ties your identity to a directory.

Sealed in transit

Relays carry only encrypted data they can't open. No one reads who, what, or when.

You are your key

Your cryptographic identity lives only on your phone. It is never uploaded, anywhere.

Don't take our word for it. AeroNyra is open source under AGPL-3.0 — read the code on GitHub and verify every claim on this page for yourself.
