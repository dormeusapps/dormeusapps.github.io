AeroNyra Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

AeroNyra is a private messenger built by DORMEUSAPPS LLC. This policy is short because we collect nothing: AeroNyra has no servers of our own, no accounts, and no analytics. It is also specific about the one thing in the path that is not ours — the public relays that carry your messages when you and a contact are apart — because a privacy policy that left them out would be wrong.

We Collect Nothing

AeroNyra has no servers of its own, no accounts, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or receive your personal information, and there is no server of ours to receive it. This is not a promise to handle your data carefully — it is an architecture with no place of ours for your data to go.

Your Identity Stays on Your Device

Your name, your photo, and your private keys live only on your phone and are never uploaded anywhere. The one thing you hand out is your public key, and you hand it out yourself: it is inside the code a contact scans in person, and inside a one-time invite you choose to send. Whoever holds an invite can use it to pair with you, so send it somewhere you trust. Phones that pass your first messages along nearby can also see it (see Nearby: Bluetooth).

Nearby: Bluetooth

When you and a contact are near each other with Bluetooth on, messages usually travel over Bluetooth between your phones, and no internet connection is needed. If Bluetooth is unavailable, or the connection drops while a message is on its way, the app may send it through the public relays instead, sealed so that they cannot read it.

When you pair nearby with an invite, the pairing message goes over Bluetooth when a Bluetooth connection is available, and stays off the relays when the two phones complete their Bluetooth handshake. If the handshake does not complete within a few seconds, or there is no Bluetooth connection, that message may be sent through the public relays instead, sealed so that they cannot read it.

When you're near other people using AeroNyra, your messages may hop through their phones to reach your contact, and theirs may hop through yours. Phones in between pass messages along without being able to read them, but they can see that a message went by, roughly how big it was, and when. The first messages to a new contact also carry your public identity key, which a phone in between could see.

Far Away: Public Relays

When you are apart, AeroNyra sends your messages through public relays on the internet. These are third-party services that DORMEUSAPPS LLC does not operate, control, or have any relationship with. The app connects to them without any account and without any authentication. A relay receives:

  • Encrypted messages it cannot read. Every message is sealed end to end on your phone before it leaves.
  • Timing and size. When each message arrives and how large it is.
  • Your IP address, as any internet connection reveals. If you use a VPN, the relay sees the VPN's address instead; nothing else changes.
  • That one connection is exchanging messages with another. A relay can see that messages pass between two connected devices; it does not see who those devices belong to.

A relay never sees your name, your photo, your private keys, or the content of any message. It holds encrypted messages for a short time so they can be delivered when your phone reconnects, and it cannot open them. Relays keep whatever records their own operators keep, under their own policies, which we neither control nor can read.

Your Messages Are End-to-End Encrypted

Every message is encrypted on your phone and decrypted only on your contact's. No one in between — not a relay, not us — can read what you say.

No Tracking, No Advertising, No Analytics

AeroNyra contains no advertising SDKs, no trackers, and no third-party analytics tools. The only third parties that ever receive anything from the app are the public relays described above, and they receive only what is listed there.

Permissions We Request, and Why

AeroNyra asks for a small number of device permissions, used only for the features they enable:

  • Bluetooth — to discover nearby devices and carry messages between phones when they are near each other.
  • Camera — to scan a contact's pairing code, for video calls, and to capture photos and videos for stories.
  • Microphone — for voice notes, calls, and the audio of videos you capture for stories.

Nothing captured through these permissions is sent to us. Photos, videos, and voice notes are sealed so that only the contact you chose can open them — sent over Bluetooth when you are near, and through the relays when you are apart or Bluetooth is unavailable.

No Recovery, by Design

Nothing readable is stored off your device, and there is no cloud backup and no account to recover. Deleting the app removes your messages and contacts from your phone, but iOS keeps your identity key in the phone's Keychain, so reinstalling AeroNyra on the same phone brings back the same identity. To destroy your identity, use Erase everything in the app's Settings. If you lose your device or reset it, your identity and your history are gone permanently. Relays hold only sealed messages, briefly, for delivery — they cannot give back anything you could read.

Nothing to Hand Over

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or disclose to anyone, including in response to legal requests. We cannot produce what we never had. Relay operators may hold encrypted messages and connection records for a time, subject to their own policies; those records do not contain your name, your keys, or the content of your messages, and we have no access to them.

Children's Privacy

AeroNyra is not intended for children under 13. Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
