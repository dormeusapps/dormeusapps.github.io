AeroNyra Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

AeroNyra is a private messenger built by DORMEUSAPPS LLC. Most privacy policies describe what a company does with the data it collects. This one is short for a simple reason: we collect nothing. AeroNyra has no servers, no accounts, and no way for your information to reach us — because there is no "us" in the loop.

We Collect Nothing

AeroNyra has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us or to anyone else. There is no server to receive it. This is not a promise to handle your data carefully — it is an architecture with no place for your data to go.

Your Identity Stays on Your Device

Your name, photo, and cryptographic identity live only on your phone. They are never uploaded. Your identity is yours alone — in AeroNyra, you are your key.

Your Messages Are End-to-End Encrypted

Messages travel directly between phones over Bluetooth, or through relays when you are apart. Relays pass along sealed data they cannot read. No one in the middle — including us — can see who you talk to, what you say, or when.

No Tracking, No Advertising, No Third Parties

AeroNyra contains no advertising SDKs, no trackers, and no third-party analytics tools. It shares nothing with third parties, because it has nothing to share and no one to share it with.

Permissions We Request, and Why

AeroNyra asks for a small number of device permissions, used only on your device for the features they enable:

  • Bluetooth — to discover nearby devices and relay messages directly between phones.
  • Camera — to scan a contact's pairing code, and for video calls.
  • Microphone — for voice notes and calls.

Nothing captured through these permissions is sent to us. There is nowhere for it to be sent.

No Recovery, by Design

Because nothing is stored off your device, if you delete the app or lose your device, your identity and data are gone permanently. There is no cloud backup and no account to recover. This is a deliberate privacy property, not a limitation — data that cannot be recovered by you also cannot be recovered by anyone else.

Nothing to Hand Over

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or disclose to anyone — including in response to legal requests. We cannot produce what we never had.

Children's Privacy

AeroNyra is not intended for children under 13. Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
